Speeduino custom v28 - flex content plausibility / high-load hold

Based directly on v27. No new idle-control changes are included in v28.

New flex-content behaviour:
- Optional high-load ethanol-content hold above configurable TPS OR RPM.
- Configurable resume delay after TPS/RPM falls back below the hold thresholds.
- Implausibly low sensor frequency/dropout holds the last accepted ethanol value.
- Sensor diagnostic/error over-frequency holds the last accepted ethanol value.
- Configurable maximum accepted ethanol-content change per second.
- Fuel temperature remains live while ethanol content is held.

Recommended starting settings:
- Hold content at high load: On
- Hold above TPS: 70%
- Hold above RPM: 4500 RPM
- Resume delay: 750 ms
- Maximum accepted content change: 5 %/s

Flex Table 2 blending:
- Fuel Table 2 blend start: E15
- Fuel Table 2 full: E70
- Spark Table 2 blend start: E20
- Spark Table 2 full: E70

This means normal E10-E12 pump fuel remains 100% on Table 1 rather than
partially blending into the ethanol tables.

speeduino 202504-flexrot-v28
