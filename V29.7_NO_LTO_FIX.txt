Speeduino custom v29.7 - AVR-GCC 12 LTO workaround

Functional ECU behaviour is unchanged from v29.6.

The v29.6 build compiled all source files successfully and failed only during
the final link/LTO stage with an AVR-GCC 12.1.0 internal compiler error in
Arduino Print.cpp:
    internal compiler error: in add_clobbers, at config/avr/avr-dimode.md

v29.7 disables Link Time Optimization for the Mega2560 environment:
- adds -flto to build_unflags
- adds -fno-lto to build_flags

This keeps AVR-GCC 12.1.0 and the current avr-libstdcpp headers, but avoids the
compiler bug in its LTO pass.

Firmware signature:
speeduino 202504-flexrot-v29.7
