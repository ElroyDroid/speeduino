Speeduino custom v29 - VSS idle entry + idle stall saver

Based directly on v28. All v28 flex-content plausibility / high-load hold and
blend-start features are retained. All v27 dashpot/PID fixes are retained.

VSS idle entry (PWM Closed+Open Loop only):
- Above the configured vehicle-speed threshold, closed-loop PID correction is
  suspended.
- The idle valve follows the normal open-loop feed-forward value, including
  dashpot, A/C idle-up and external idle-up additions.
- The PID integral is reset while vehicle speed remains above the threshold.
- At/below the threshold, closed-loop PID resumes.
- The feature only acts when Speeduino VSS itself is configured/enabled.

Recommended starting value:
- VSS idle entry: On
- Enable closed loop at/below: 5 kph

Idle stall saver (PWM Closed Loop and PWM Closed+Open Loop):
- Active only with engine running and TPS below the configured maximum.
- Begins adding emergency airflow when RPM falls below idle target by the
  configured Start amount.
- Progressively moves the commanded valve duty toward the Rescue Duty.
- Reaches full Rescue Duty at the configured Full amount below target.
- It only increases valve duty; it never reduces the normal PID command.
- Rotational-idle and overheat IAC overrides retain priority.

Recommended starting values:
- Stall saver: On
- Start: 100 RPM below target
- Full: 300 RPM below target
- Rescue duty: 60%
- Maximum TPS: 1.5%

speeduino 202504-flexrot-v29
